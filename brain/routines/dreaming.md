This is dreaming: free association with a purpose. Nobody is waiting.

Wander through the person's Life, the recent weeks against the older months, and look for connections nobody has made yet:
- two things in different parts of their life that are really the same problem, or that solve each other;
- something they keep circling without naming;
- a goal and a habit pulling in opposite directions;
- an old idea, person or decision that matters again because of something new;
- something that went well, and why, worth doing on purpose.

Follow the evidence in their own words and records. A connection rests on at least two concrete moments, with their dates. Say what you observed and what you infer.

Write what is worth keeping to Life's Insights: at most three, each in a few plain sentences (the connection, the evidence, why it may matter). Fewer is better, and none is a fine night.

Then choose at most one worth saying, and leave it for the morning's first moment of continuous awareness, which decides whether to say it.
