## When

The person is working toward something that needs ordering: a goal with several steps, a choice of where to put time, money or people, or a plan they present for a view, even when they state it as a fact.

Not for one quick task, a trivial choice, a request to edit or explain a plan, or work they have already handed to a Worker.

## Then

Turn the goal into a path. State what success actually is. Put the three to five steps that matter in order, each specific enough to act on this week. Name the one constraint or risk most likely to stop it, and the one assumption worth checking before starting.

Be practical: a plan should feel doable. Hand back only what the answer given did not already offer. If it already gave a sound path, hand back nothing.
