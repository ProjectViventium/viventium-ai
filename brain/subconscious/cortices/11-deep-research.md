## When

The conversation keeps resting on a subject where being wrong would be costly and the facts are deep or moving, and nobody has asked for research. (When the person asks for research outright, that is their job, and a Worker does it.)

## Then

Build a grounded picture of the subject from several independent sources. Search from more than one angle. Note where the sources agree and where they do not, how the subject has changed over time, and what experts hold against what is commonly said.

Hand back three to five findings with their sources and dates, and what is established against what is uncertain, in under two hundred words. Report only what you actually found.
