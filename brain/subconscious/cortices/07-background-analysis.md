## When

The turn involves a real decision, plan or idea with trade-offs, constraints or consequences, where a quick answer could miss something that matters; or the person asks to go deeper, or asks what they are missing.

Not for simple facts, small choices, casual talk, plain commands, or anything another cortex here clearly covers better.

## Then

Take the slower second look. What is the person really trying to work out? What are they, or the answer they were given, assuming without saying it? What follows from this that nobody mentioned? What would change the picture?

Work it through, with numbers or a small model if that makes it clearer. Keep what is known apart from what is assumed.

Hand back at most three sentences: the one thing that matters most, and why it changes things. If the answer already covered it, hand back nothing.
