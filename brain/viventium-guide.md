# Viventium AI by xPerfect.ai

## What it does

Viventium is a personal AI and second brain on your Mac. The conversation stays in one place when you switch between typing and talking. Its Life folder and Memory page let you keep context across conversations and correct or forget what you shared. Workers, when set up, can do longer tasks while you keep talking.

## Settings

Open Settings with the gear under the V in the macOS menu bar, or from the conversation. In Settings, go to Personality to choose a style. Go to Feelings to change how Viventium reacts. Use Voice to select how it listens, speaks and sounds. The available choices depend on your Mac and the accounts you connected. In System, you can connect more accounts or enter API keys. You can change where Viventium runs at the top of Settings.

## Controls

Click the V to open the menu-bar panel. Ears controls the microphone and spoken conversation. With it off, you can keep typing. Talk is the regular spoken conversation, Wing listens and replies when you address Viventium, and Listen only listens without speaking. Use Wing when you want a sidekick on a call. Turn off powers Viventium down until you open it again. The conversation has its own microphone and mode controls when voice is available.

## Stored on this Mac

Viventium AI saves its conversation history, Life notes and settings on the Mac. You can correct or forget memories in Settings. Online services can still receive conversation or memory context for a request they process.

## Processing

A part set to run on this Mac processes its input here. The Local profile uses on-device models for its supported parts. Check Settings for each part and its backup.

Hybrid and Cloud can send inputs to the services selected in Settings. For example, an online mind receives your words and relevant conversation or memory context. Online transcription receives your audio. An online voice service receives the text it speaks. Each service handles its input under its own policies.

An online Worker can receive the task and the context or files it needs. A connected service receives the data needed for the action it performs. Telegram conversations also pass through Telegram.
